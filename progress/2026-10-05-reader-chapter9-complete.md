## Explicit qualifications

- The path-algebra dimension theorem assumes finitely many vertices. The
  path-count comparison additionally assumes the Hom-space identifications
  instead of constructing the canonical projective covers.
- The semisimple example's selected result classifies linking classes; it does
  not itself construct a block equivalence with vector spaces.
- The generator criterion assumes P is already projective. The printed
  exercise does not state this necessary qualification.
- The unit construction uses the full projective-generator hypotheses, not
  just the weaker nonzero-projective hypothesis suggested by the hint.
- The combined basic-representative theorem starts from an algebra over an
  algebraically closed field and uses k-linear equivalences of all-module
  categories. It does not directly state the category-quantified version or
  uniqueness under arbitrary equivalences of finite-module categories.

The finite-term resolution may continue indefinitely; the projective dth
syzygy is what permits truncation to length d. Do not confuse finite terms
with finite length. The projective-cover record here includes indecomposability,
so it is not presented as a general cover definition for decomposable modules.
